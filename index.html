physics-experiments-lab

Ten interactive experiments. You touch, move and measure, and real numbers change in front of you.

The experiment pages are in English (a first translation, not yet read by a native speaker; a few long explanatory passages are still in Italian). The live copy on the site is in Italian: developcontroller.com/laboratorio. Experiment 3 uses a real microwave oven, a chocolate bar and a ruler.
  1. 01 · How small does a vortex get?Stir harder and watch the smallest eddy: its size, speed and lifetime. The scales follow from viscosity and the energy you put in.
  2. 02 · How much does the ambulance note jump?Move the siren with your finger and compare the pitch before and after it passes.
  3. 03 · The speed of light, with a rulerMeasure the spacing of the melted spots in a microwave oven, read its frequency, multiply.
  4. 04 · Weighing a planet with a stopwatchThe time a moon takes to go around gives the mass of the planet it orbits.
  5. 05 · How big can an animal getScale an ant up and see why weight grows faster than the strength that has to carry it.
  6. 06 · How long chance takes to cross youA random walk across a cell, a metre: doubling the distance costs four times the time.
  7. 07 · How many times you must stir before the air all goes to the leftEntropy as counting: four molecules, a breath, a room.
  8. 08 · How far a forecast getsTwo almost identical starting points tell completely different stories after a few steps.
  9. 09 · How many muons are passing through your hand right nowTime dilation: why muons made 15 km up reach the ground.
  10. 10 · How fast something closed in a box movesThe smaller the box, the faster what is inside it must move.
